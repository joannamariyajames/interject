# Disruptions & Support

## Delays
A delay above 3 hours entitles the traveller to a meal voucher and, on the next
day departure, a hotel night. Claim it at the airline desk before leaving the
airport; retroactive claims are refused.

## Missed connections
Protected itineraries rebook automatically on the next available departure.
Separately ticketed segments are not protected and must be rebooked and paid for
again, then claimed against travel insurance.

## Contact
The 24-hour desk answers in under 90 seconds on the priority line. Web chat runs
07:00 to 23:00 local. Email responses target 8 business hours.

## Insurance
The corporate policy covers trip interruption up to 150,000 INR, lost baggage up
to 60,000 INR, and medical up to 2,000,000 INR abroad. It does not cover
adventure sports or pre-existing conditions declared after booking.
