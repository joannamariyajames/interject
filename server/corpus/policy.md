# Travel Policy & Expenses

## Approval thresholds
Domestic trips under 40,000 INR total are auto-approved. Anything above needs a
manager sign-off recorded before ticketing. International travel always needs
sign-off regardless of amount.

## Per diem
Metro cities are 2,600 INR per day, other cities 1,800 INR per day. Per diem is
halved on days where a conference provides meals.

## Reimbursement
Claims must be filed within 30 days of trip completion. Receipts are required
above 1,000 INR. Ride-hailing receipts must show the pickup and drop addresses.
Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Refunds
Refunds on cancelled bookings land back on the original payment method in 5 to 7
business days. Refunds to a corporate card go to the company account, not to the
traveller.
